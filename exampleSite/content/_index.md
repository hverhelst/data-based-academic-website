 Hi, I'm Alex Rivera, an assistant professor in the Department of Applied Mathematics at the Northern Institute of Technology.

 This is the demo site for the [Data-based Academic Website](https://github.com/hverhelst/data-based-academic-website) theme. Everything below this paragraph — every section, in the order it appears — is rendered from JSON files in `data/`, and none of it required writing a template. The **Grants & Funding** section is the interesting one: it has no template of its own anywhere in the theme, only an entry in `data/sections.json` describing which fields to show.
